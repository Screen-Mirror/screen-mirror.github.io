How It Works

Step 1: Connect

Plug your iPhone or iPad into your Mac using a USB cable. Launch Screen Mirror for instant detection.

Step 2: Mirror

Your device's screen appears on your Mac display. Audio streams through your Mac speakers automatically.

Step 3: Control

Use your Mac's keyboard and mouse to interact fully. Switch apps, type, and navigate with ease.

Step 4: Share or Enjoy

Record, share in calls, or watch privately. Disconnect anytime—your workflow stays uninterrupted.

FAQ

Is a Wi-Fi connection required?

No. A simple USB cable ensures stable, low-latency mirroring with full audio and control.

Does it support audio?

Yes. All device audio plays through your Mac, ideal for videos, games, and calls.

Can I control my device from the Mac?

Fully. Keyboard and mouse input mirrors precisely to your iPhone or iPad.

What devices are compatible?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 11 or later. Works on macOS Ventura and newer.
